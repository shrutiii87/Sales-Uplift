# Sales Uplift: Strategy Insights from Multi-Region Retail Data 📊✨

=============================================================

• 👑 C8600 is the top customer at ₹67,264 in total purchases — top 5 customers are strong candidates for a loyalty program.

• 📱🎧 Smartphones and Headphones are the highest-revenue products (~₹0.5M each) — prioritize for Q3 promotions and inventory.

• 🌐 Online outperforms Offline, especially in Electronics and Beauty — shift more premium marketing to digital channels.

• 📉 All categories dip in Q2–Q3 every year — a mid-year campaign or discount push could reduce this seasonal drop.

• 🗺️ North and West regions underperform in premium products — targeted marketing needed; South and East are already strong.

• 🔁 Repeat buyers (10+ purchases) are a high-value segment — ideal for VIP offers or exclusive deals to boost retention.
